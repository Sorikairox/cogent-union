These are the section's current demands. Each one was decided in a section
meeting, and each is updated as negotiations move.

## Pay

1. A pay rise that at least matches inflation, applied to every pay grade.
2. Publication of the pay ranges for every role, so that gaps can be seen and discussed.
3. Overtime recorded and paid for everyone, with no "flat rate" exceptions.

## Working time

1. A real right to disconnect outside working hours, written into company policy.
2. Advance notice for schedule changes, and compensation when that notice is not given.

## Respect at work

1. A harassment procedure that does not route complaints through the person's own manager.
2. Union representation available in every disciplinary meeting.

*Where a demand has been accepted, rejected or is still under discussion will be
noted here as each negotiation closes.*

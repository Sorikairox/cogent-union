These are the demands the section submitted to the company on 30 October 2026,
together with a request for collective bargaining.

They are sorted by something that is not obvious from the outside: **whether we
can legally make the company sit down and discuss them with us.** That depends
on who is in the union, not on how good the demand is.

## What we can't discuss — and want to

> **We cannot bargain over this, because we have no members in the departments
> it affects.** A company is only obliged to negotiate with a union over the
> working conditions of the people that union represents. One colleague from
> the affected team joining changes that immediately.
>
> **[Join the union — contact us](mailto:cogentlabsunion@gmail.com)** ·
> Membership is confidential, and nothing is shared with management.

- **Restore flex-time for the Sales team.** Sales is currently required in the
  office at 8:30 every day. We have no Sales members, so the company has no
  obligation to discuss it with us.

## What we can discuss — but need more weight behind it

We can put this on the table. Whether it moves depends on how many of us are
behind it.

- **More remote days, for everyone.** Our filed demand: keep one come-to-office
  day a week and let each person choose office or remote for the other days, on
  the premise of internal team adjustment and sharing with the manager.

If you want this, the useful thing is not agreeing quietly — it is joining, so
that the number across the table is bigger.

## What we can and will discuss

These are on the agenda and we can hold the company to them.

- **Train managers properly.** Introduce a more effective training system for
  managers, aimed at preventing harassment and bullying — real participatory
  training, not a passive PowerPoint presentation.
- **Remind everyone of the whistleblower system, monthly.** Through a regular
  meeting or a company-wide email.
- **Say where the company stands financially, every Friday.** Share the
  financial situation at the Friday all-hands, including how far we are from
  profitability, so that people can do better work and choose their tasks more
  efficiently.
- **Disclose the cost centres in detail.** Rent, total salaries per department,
  third-party vendors, infrastructure.

## Where this stands

We have asked the company for a collective bargaining session on **30 November
2026, 19:00 to 21:00**, in a meeting room at the company, with interpreters on
both sides.

**That date is our proposal, not a fixture.** The company has not answered yet,
so nothing about the time, the place or the session itself is settled. We will
say so here when it is.

Once the session happens we will publish what was agreed, refused, or left
open — the same way we publish everything else.

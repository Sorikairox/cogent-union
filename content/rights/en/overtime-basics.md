> This guide is written by the section for orientation. It is not legal advice,
> and it does not replace your contract or the collective agreement that applies
> to you.

## The rule

Hours worked beyond your contractual working time are overtime. They must be
recorded, and they must be paid at a premium rate. Neither "we are a flat-rate
company" nor "everyone does it" removes that obligation.

## Check your own numbers

1. Write down your actual start and end times for one full month.
2. Compare the total with the hours on your payslip.
3. Keep the evidence: schedules, messages asking you to stay, access badge logs.

## If the numbers do not match

Ask HR in writing for a breakdown — a written request creates a dated record.
Send the section a copy. You do not have to go through this alone, and a claim
made by several colleagues together carries much more weight than one made alone.

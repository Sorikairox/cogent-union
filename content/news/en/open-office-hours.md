From this month the section holds open office hours **every Thursday, 12:00 to
14:00**. No appointment needed, no membership needed.

Bring a contract you do not understand, a schedule that does not add up, or a
conversation with a manager that left you uneasy. If we cannot answer on the
spot, we will say so and come back to you with a checked answer.

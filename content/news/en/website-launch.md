Posters fall off walls and chat messages scroll away. This site is the section's
durable record: every statement we publish stays reachable, dated and readable
in both languages.

## How it is maintained

Every article on this site is a file in a public Git repository. Nothing is
published without being reviewed, and the full history of changes is visible to
anyone who wants to check it.

## What comes next

Practical guides on your rights, the section's demands, and background dossiers
on the issues that keep coming back at work.

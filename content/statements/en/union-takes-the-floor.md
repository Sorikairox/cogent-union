The section is growing. A new union representative has been appointed, and this
website is now live — two steps that serve the same commitment: **to inform, to
defend, to act**, with you and never without you.

## What changes for you

- A single place to find our statements, our demands and practical guides on your rights.
- Every page in English and Japanese, so that nobody is left out of the conversation.
- A direct way to reach us when something goes wrong at work.

## What does not change

Our method. We check facts before we publish them, we say what we know and what
we do not, and we take no position in your name without asking you first.

> A union is not a service desk. It is what we build together, meeting by
> meeting, question by question.

## Get in touch

Member or not, write to the section. Every message is read, and nothing you tell
us is shared with management without your explicit agreement.
